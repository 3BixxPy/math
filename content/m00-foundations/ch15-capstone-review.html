Module 0 Capstone — Mixed Review & Diagnostic Retake

No new material today. Two things: an interleaved mixed problem set (topics shuffled — you have to identify the right technique yourself, not just apply whatever the chapter title told you to), and a retake of the exact problems from the diagnostic that started this whole curriculum.

Why mixed, not grouped by topic

Every problem set so far told you which technique to use, by being in that chapter. Real problems don't come labeled. This is where you find out what actually stuck versus what only worked while the chapter title was giving it away — the single most reliable way to test whether something moved into durable memory.

Mixed review (all of Module 0)

  1. Factor: x² − 7x + 12
    \((x-3)(x-4)\)
  2. Simplify, with restriction: (x² − 16)/(x − 4)
    \(x+4\), \(x\neq4\)
  3. Solve: 4(x − 3) + 5 = 2x + 1
    \(4x-7=2x+1 \Rightarrow x=4\)
  4. Simplify: 3⁻² · 3⁵
    \(3^3=27\)
  5. Solve (either method): x² + x − 6 = 0
    \((x+3)(x-2)=0 \Rightarrow x=-3,2\)
  6. Simplify: (2x³)⁻²
    \(\dfrac{1}{4x^6}\)
  7. Factor by grouping: x³ + 4x² + 2x + 8
    \((x+4)(x^2+2)\)
  8. Simplify: √98
    \(7\sqrt2\)
  9. Add, with restrictions: 1/(x+2) + 1/(x−2)
    \(\dfrac{2x}{(x+2)(x-2)}\), \(x\neq2,-2\)
  10. Solve the system by elimination: 2x + y = 9, x − y = 3
    \(x=4, y=1\)
  11. Solve: −3x + 4 ≤ 16
    \(x\ge-4\)
  12. Solve by completing the square: x² − 6x + 4 = 0
    \((x-3)^2=5 \Rightarrow x=3\pm\sqrt5\)

Diagnostic retake

The exact algebra problems from your original diagnostic (the ones that shaped this whole curriculum), side by side with what you got then.

1. Solve: 3(x − 4) + 2 = 5x − 6

Your original answer: x = 2 (from an expansion slip: 3x−2 instead of 3x−10)

Correct answer: \(x=-2\). See Chapters 1 and 12 if this doesn't come immediately.

2. Simplify: (x² − 9) / (x − 3)

Your original approach: term-by-term guesswork, no result

Correct answer: \(x+3\), \(x\neq3\). See Chapters 6, 7, and 10.

3. Solve: x² − 5x + 6 = 0

Your original answer: x = 2, 3 — correct, but you had to look up the formula

Still \(x=2,3\) — now derivable by factoring (Chapter 8) in one line, or from the formula rebuilt from scratch (Chapter 13).

4. Simplify: 2⁻³ · 2⁵, and (a²b³)²

Your original answers: "no idea" for the first; a⁵b⁶ for the second (miscounted by hand)

Correct answers: \(2^{-3}\cdot2^5=4\) (Chapter 3), and \((a^2b^3)^2=a^4b^6\) (Chapter 4).

(The functions/graphs, trig, and log questions from the original diagnostic get their own retake at the end of Modules 1, 2, and 3 respectively, once that material's been built.)
